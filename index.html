About us

Emily Cleans is a trusted, personal cleaning service across Chester and the Wirral. I started the business alone, and now Megan works alongside me — together we deliver careful, five-star cleans for family homes, elderly clients, and salons throughout the area.

Whether you're after a regular cleaner for a busy family home, someone your parents feel comfortable having round each week, or a salon that needs to look spotless before opening, we're happy to help.

With two experienced, fully insured cleaners who get to know your space and your routine, no home is too particular and no job too small for Emily Cleans.

Our Services

Home Cleaning

Keep your home feeling fresh and tidy with regular weekly, fortnightly or one-off cleaning. We can tailor each clean around your home and your individual needs.

Eco-Friendly Cleaning

A more environmentally conscious way to keep your space clean. We use eco-friendly cleaning products where possible, helping you enjoy a beautifully clean home while being kinder to the environment.

Salon & Beauty Studio Cleaning

Professional cleaning for salons, beauty rooms and studios, helping you maintain a fresh, hygienic and welcoming environment for your clients and staff.

Deep Cleaning

A more thorough clean for those areas that need some extra attention. Perfect for giving your home or workspace a proper refresh from top to bottom.
